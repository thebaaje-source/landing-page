Sony WH-1000XM5 review: excellent headphones for everyday listening

By Rhea Sen • August 21, 2026 • 6 min read

The Sony WH-1000XM5 are comfortable wireless headphones with strong noise cancellation, clear calls and a warm, enjoyable sound. They are a very good choice for travel, work and daily music listening.

What is good

The noise cancellation is the biggest strength. It reduces the low sound of buses, trains and airplanes very effectively, so you can listen at a lower volume. The headphones are also light at 250 grams, and the soft ear cushions remain comfortable during long sessions.

Music sounds detailed without becoming harsh. The Sony Headphones Connect app gives you an equalizer, adaptive sound control and a few useful listening settings. Call quality is good in quiet places and acceptable in normal office environments.

What could be better

The WH-1000XM5 do not fold as small as the older WH-1000XM4, which makes the case a little larger. The touch controls work well, but some people may prefer physical buttons. At Sony's list price of $399.99, they are expensive, although sale prices often make them easier to recommend.

Key specifications

  • Battery: Up to 30 hours with noise cancellation
  • Weight: 250 grams
  • Connection: Bluetooth 5.2, USB-C and 3.5mm cable
  • Audio: LDAC, AAC and SBC support
  • Charging: 3 minutes provides up to 3 hours of playback

Verdict

The Sony WH-1000XM5 are a polished pair of headphones with excellent noise cancellation, comfortable design and dependable sound. Buy them if you travel often or want a quiet focus space.
